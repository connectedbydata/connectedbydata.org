### What I’ve been doing
I’ve realised that I was somewhat naive in my presumptions about what [winding up an organisation](https://connectedbydata.org/blog/2026/03/30/winding-up) would look like. I had thought that as we approached the final six months that we’d be finishing off projects; catching up on all the loose ends we’ve created over the last 4-5 years; putting some effort into some work that needs to be done but we haven’t had explicit funding to do before - and then some organisational administration around closing whilst celebrating what we’ve achieved and some bits around legacy.

I was wrong.

We’re still starting new work - in Tim’s case really big stuff and in Jeni’s more rapid delivery bits. We hosted a one day in person conference next week (with all the activity that goes into that) and we’re still supporting PAIRS as PAIRS 2027 starts gearing up.

In part this is because as a result of our exploring regeneration options we’re focusing on whether there is a role for Connected by Data as some form of incubator for invited projects - allowing the benefit of the organisational infrastructure to be continued whilst the name / public profile of the organisation will close. In practice that means I’m in essence:
* celebrating/legacy work for the end of one organisation;
* working out what’s needed (and laying some non-committal groundwork) to transition into an incubator type model;
* providing operations support for PAIRS - including the planning of PAIRS 2027;
* supporting the setup of a new organisation (programme incubated within Connected by Data) with global partners / delivery and multiple projects;
* continuing my contributions to ongoing/new Connected by Data delivery and organisational operations.

Needless to say, I'm busy. And honestly I’m not sure how well anyone thinks I’m doing any one of those at the moment. I’m certainly nowhere near on top of things (and some of them are trying to move very rapidly). 

I spent a bit of time worrying that I was losing my speed - which I usually consider a strength - but I’ve decided that it is fair to be busy and a bit behind on 3 days a week.

### What I need to take care of
See above! I’ve got lots of partnership agreements and data sharing agreements to write / wrangle / get signed. I’ve got incubator structure questions to wrangle and make proposals around. These are the priorities right now.
