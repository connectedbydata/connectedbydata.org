AI is reshaping work, and the union movement has a critical role in representing workers through this change: protecting their rights, ensuring they have a voice, and securing a share in the gains. Unions must be able to respond to the complex challenges created by AI. However, their ability to do so is not consistent across sectors or unions. The TUC is seeking to address this challenge by providing resources that can help unions build capacity on AI.
