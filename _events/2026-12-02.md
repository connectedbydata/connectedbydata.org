This year’s Summit will ask whether we have the right institutions, bodies, and governance structures to address where we are today and apply foresight to what may lie ahead. We will explore whether the digital ethics principles and frameworks being put into action are delivering the outcomes they were designed to achieve, and what more may still be needed?
