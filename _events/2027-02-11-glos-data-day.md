We have supported previous Gloucestershire Data Days as a local, place based, example of convening around data. 

<!--more-->

Glos Data Day returns for its third edition in 2027, bringing people from across Gloucestershire together to make sense of their stories and put them to work for the county. Create Data grows this event into a strand of our work to energise a data movement in Gloucestershire: more gatherings, more shared knowledge across systems, and growing support for communities to understand and use their own data to shape the decisions that affect them.

Our next Data Day, taking place on 11 February 2027, explores how data, creativity and connection can help us navigate a changing world. Drawing on themes of nature, resilience and collective action, we'll consider how data acts as a shared root system: helping us understand what's happening around us, connect across communities and sectors, and make informed decisions together in the face of uncertainty.

Bringing together people from across Gloucestershire, the day will create space to learn, share and imagine how data can support a more connected, resilient and thriving county.

For more information visit the [Gloucestershire Data Day website](https://www.creategloucestershire.co.uk/opportunity/call-for-contributions-gloucestershire-data-day-2027)
